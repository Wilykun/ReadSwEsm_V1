name: AntiTagBot reply tone
description: Keep AntiTagBot outcome replies funny and emoji-friendly without obscuring what happened.
---

Use light, context-matched humor and emojis in AntiTagBot replies for successful deletions, owner/admin exemptions, missing bot permissions, uncertain status, and failed deletions. Keep the outcome explicit and factual; only claim a message was deleted after the delete operation succeeds. Owners and group admins remain exempt from deletion.

**Why:** The user wants the bot's replies to be funny while remaining accurate about deletion and role-based exemptions.

**How to apply:** Put the verified outcome first, then add a short joke and suitable emoji; never let humor imply an action that did not happen.
